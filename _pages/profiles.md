## join?

We're always interested to hear from prospective lab members. We are a data-driven computational lab with strong ties to wet-lab and field collaborators. Given that our work combines theory and data analysis, an interest in biological applications of programming and statistics is key (but no previous experience is required). If interested in our lab, please contact Rafael via email.

Postdoc candidates: include a copy of your CV and a brief description of your research interests.

Graduate students: consider applying through the [Genetics and Genomics Scholars](https://ggscholars.org/) program, or the [Biology, Genetics, or Bioinformatics](https://bio.sciences.ncsu.edu/graduate/) graduate programs at the Department of Biological Sciences.
