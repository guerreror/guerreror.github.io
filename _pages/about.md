Welcome to the homepage of the Guerrero Lab in the Department of Biological Sciences at North Carolina State University.

Our research uses a combination of data analysis and modeling in population genetics (with a little bit of data collection, predominantly through collaborative efforts), to study the mechanisms underlying genetic variation in nature and quantify evolutionary forces at play.
